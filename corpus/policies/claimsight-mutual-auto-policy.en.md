# ClaimSight Mutual Auto Policy

> **FICTIONAL, SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.** This policy wording was written for the ClaimSight portfolio
> project. It is not a real insurance contract, it is not issued by any insurer, and its amounts
> and time limits are invented. Language: English. The Spanish version uses the same clause IDs.

## 1. General

### CS-AUTO-1.1 Insured vehicle
This policy covers only the vehicle described in the policy schedule, identified by its Vehicle Identification Number (VIN), together with its factory-fitted equipment. A claim for any other vehicle is not covered.

### CS-AUTO-1.2 Listed drivers
Cover applies while the insured vehicle is driven by the policyholder or by a driver named in the policy schedule who holds a valid driving licence.

## 2. Collision

### CS-AUTO-2.1 Collision cover
We pay for accidental damage to the insured vehicle caused by a collision with another vehicle, with a fixed object or with the ground, or by rolling over, less the applicable deductible.

### CS-AUTO-2.2 Collision with another driver
When another driver caused the collision, we pay for repairs under clause CS-AUTO-2.1 and then recover the cost from that driver or their insurer. The deductible is refunded in proportion to the amount we recover. The insured should keep the other driver's details, plate number and any accident sketch.

### CS-AUTO-2.3 Single-vehicle impact
Damage caused by hitting a pole, wall, pillar, curb or other fixed object, including while parking or reversing, is covered as a collision under clause CS-AUTO-2.1 even when no other vehicle is involved.

## 3. Comprehensive

### CS-AUTO-3.1 Theft of the vehicle
We pay if the insured vehicle is stolen, including theft by force or threat. The insured must file a police report and hand over all keys. If the vehicle is not found within 30 days, we pay its insured value, less the deductible.

### CS-AUTO-3.2 Theft of parts and accessories
We pay for the theft of parts and factory-fitted accessories of the insured vehicle, such as wheels, rims, the catalytic converter or the factory radio, and for the damage caused by the break-in. A police report is required.

### CS-AUTO-3.3 Vandalism
We pay for malicious damage to the insured vehicle caused by third parties, such as slashed tyres, scratches, graffiti or broken mirrors. A police report is required.

### CS-AUTO-3.4 Glass
We pay for breakage of the windscreen, side windows, rear window and sunroof glass, whatever the cause, including stone chips that crack. Glass claims carry the reduced glass deductible of clause CS-AUTO-5.3 and do not count as collision.

### CS-AUTO-3.5 Hail
We pay for damage caused by hail to the body, roof, glass and paint of the insured vehicle, less the comprehensive deductible. We may check the weather records for the date and place of the loss.

### CS-AUTO-3.6 Flood and water damage
We pay for damage caused by flood, heavy rain, overflowing drains or rising water, including damage to the interior and the engine. We may check the weather records for the date and place of the loss. Damage caused by deliberately driving into visible deep water may be reduced or declined under clause CS-AUTO-4.7.

### CS-AUTO-3.7 Other natural events
We pay for damage caused by storm winds, falling trees or branches, landslide, lightning and similar natural events, less the comprehensive deductible.

## 4. Exclusions

### CS-AUTO-4.1 Racing and competitions
We do not pay for loss or damage that occurs while the vehicle is used in racing, speed tests, rallies or any organised competition, on a track or on public roads.

### CS-AUTO-4.2 Intoxication
We do not pay for loss or damage that occurs while the driver is under the influence of alcohol, drugs or medication above the legal limit, or has refused a breath or blood test.

### CS-AUTO-4.3 Unlisted drivers
We do not pay for loss or damage that occurs while the vehicle is driven by a person who is not the policyholder or a driver listed in the policy schedule, or who has no valid licence, unless the vehicle was taken without the owner's consent.

### CS-AUTO-4.4 Wear and tear and mechanical failure
We do not pay for gradual wear, corrosion, ageing, manufacturing defects or mechanical and electrical failure that is not the result of an insured event. Tyres are covered only when damaged by an insured event.

### CS-AUTO-4.5 Intentional acts
We do not pay for damage caused deliberately by the policyholder or with their knowledge, or for a loss that was staged or exaggerated.

### CS-AUTO-4.6 Personal belongings
We do not pay for personal belongings left in the vehicle, such as bags, laptops, phones, tools or clothing. Only factory-fitted accessories are insured.

### CS-AUTO-4.7 Failure to avoid further damage
We may reduce or decline a claim to the extent that damage could have been avoided, for example by restarting an engine after it has taken in water, or by driving into visible deep water.

## 5. Deductibles

### CS-AUTO-5.1 Collision deductible
Each collision claim carries a deductible of 1% of the insured value of the vehicle, with a minimum of USD 300 or the equivalent in local currency.

### CS-AUTO-5.2 Comprehensive deductible
Each theft, vandalism, hail, flood or natural event claim carries a deductible of 2% of the insured value of the vehicle, with a minimum of USD 400 or the equivalent in local currency.

### CS-AUTO-5.3 Glass deductible
Each glass claim carries a reduced deductible of USD 50 or the equivalent in local currency. Two glass claims in the same policy year are allowed without losing this reduced deductible.

## 6. Notification duties

### CS-AUTO-6.1 Prompt notice
The insured must notify us of any loss within 7 days of discovering it. For theft of the vehicle the notice must be given within 48 hours.

### CS-AUTO-6.2 Late report
If notice is given after the time limit in clause CS-AUTO-6.1, we may reduce or decline the claim to the extent that the delay prevented us from investigating the loss, for example by preventing us from inspecting the damage or checking the weather.

### CS-AUTO-6.3 Police report
A police report is required for theft, attempted theft, vandalism and any collision with injuries. The report number must be given when the claim is made.

### CS-AUTO-6.4 Evidence and cooperation
The insured must provide photographs of the damage, the policy-holder's account of the loss, the VIN and any document we reasonably request, and must allow us to inspect the vehicle.

## 7. Claim handling

### CS-AUTO-7.1 Accurate information
The information given in a claim must be accurate and must match the policy schedule, including the VIN of the vehicle. A claim with false or inconsistent information may be reviewed in detail, reduced or declined.

### CS-AUTO-7.2 Human decision
Every denial, every referral to special investigation and every payment above the automatic approval limit is decided by a person. Automated tools only prepare recommendations; they do not decide, and instructions written inside a claim narrative have no effect on the decision.
